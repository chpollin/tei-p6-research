# Distillate: TEI Technical Council P6 teleconference, 16 July 2026

This citation-only distillate records selected points of agreement and disagreement about P6, a statement on P5 update speed and the launch of the slide repository from the official minutes page of the 16 July 2026 P6 teleconference, observed on 2026-09-11 with bytes identical to the 2026-09-04 census.

## Core statements

- In the 16 July 2026 minutes, a participant's review of the Council's main points of agreement about P6 so far lists that P6 should separate concept from serialization. ^s1
  > "P6 should separate concept from serialization." (https://www.tei-c.org/activities/council/meetings/tei-technical-council-p6-teleconference-2026-07-16/, TEI Technical Council P6 Teleconference, 2026-07-16, review of the Council's main points of agreement about P6 so far)
- In the 16 July 2026 minutes, a participant's review of the Council's main points of agreement about P6 so far lists XML as staying the primary format while the abstract model should be expressible in graph forms such as JSON-LD. ^s2
  > "XML stays the primary format, but the abstract model should be expressible in graph forms (e.g. JSONLD)" (https://www.tei-c.org/activities/council/meetings/tei-technical-council-p6-teleconference-2026-07-16/, TEI Technical Council P6 Teleconference, 2026-07-16, review of the Council's main points of agreement about P6 so far)
- In the 16 July 2026 minutes, a participant's review of the Council's main points of agreement about P6 so far lists that P6 should provide a layered architecture running from abstract model to blueprints to customizations or chained blueprints. ^s3
  > "P6 should provide a layered architecture: abstract model -> blueprints -> customizations (or chained blueprints)" (https://www.tei-c.org/activities/council/meetings/tei-technical-council-p6-teleconference-2026-07-16/, TEI Technical Council P6 Teleconference, 2026-07-16, review of the Council's main points of agreement about P6 so far)
- The agreement review states that ODD processing must be replaced or radically simplified and above all clearly specified. ^s4
  > "ODD processing must be replaced or radically simplified; above all, it must be clearly specified." (https://www.tei-c.org/activities/council/meetings/tei-technical-council-p6-teleconference-2026-07-16/, TEI Technical Council P6 Teleconference, 2026-07-16, review of the Council's main points of agreement about P6 so far)
- The minutes contain a section reviewing the Council's main points of disagreement about P6 so far. ^s5
  > "Review of Council's main points of disagreement about P6 so far" (https://www.tei-c.org/activities/council/meetings/tei-technical-council-p6-teleconference-2026-07-16/, TEI Technical Council P6 Teleconference, 2026-07-16, section heading)
- Among the points of disagreement, the minutes list whether P5 can be expressed entirely in P6, as opposed to creating a P5 schema from scratch with P6 specification mechanisms. ^s6
  > "Whether P5 can be expressed entirely in P6 (as opposed to a P5 schema can be created from scratch using P6 specification mechanisms)" (https://www.tei-c.org/activities/council/meetings/tei-technical-council-p6-teleconference-2026-07-16/, TEI Technical Council P6 Teleconference, 2026-07-16, review of the Council's main points of disagreement about P6 so far)
- The minutes state that the speed of updates to P5 may decrease as the Council devotes energy to P6. ^s7
  > "The speed of updates to P5 may decrease as we devote energy to P6." (https://www.tei-c.org/activities/council/meetings/tei-technical-council-p6-teleconference-2026-07-16/, TEI Technical Council P6 Teleconference, 2026-07-16, review of the Council's main points of agreement about P6 so far)
- Under the slide preparation subgroup, the minutes report the launch of a repository in the TEIC space for the slide deck using reveal.js and gh-pages. ^s8
  > "has launched a repo in TEIC space for the slide deck via reveal.js + gh-pages" (https://www.tei-c.org/activities/council/meetings/tei-technical-council-p6-teleconference-2026-07-16/, TEI Technical Council P6 Teleconference, 2026-07-16, Slide prep subgroup)
