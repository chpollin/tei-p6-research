# Distillate: Council session “It’s Time for P6!” at TEI 2026 on 13 August 2026, slides at commit eb924226

This citation-only distillate records what the Technical Council's TEI 2026 slide source `index.html` states about P6 at the pinned repository commit `eb924226d12d22599bae9dad4fc53bc748b3f121`.

## Core statements

- The slide source identifies the session as taking place at TEI 2026 on Thursday 13 August at 1 pm in Vancouver. ^s1
  > "TEI 2026: Thurs. 13 August @ 1pm, Vancouver, BC" (https://github.com/TEIC/timeForP6/blob/eb924226d12d22599bae9dad4fc53bc748b3f121/index.html, title slide)
- Under what the Council wants for P6, the slides state that TEI will still be encoded in XML but should also be readily expressible in other formats such as graph models. ^s2
  > "We will still encode in XML, but TEI should also be readily expressible in other formats (e.g. graph models)." (https://github.com/TEIC/timeForP6/blob/eb924226d12d22599bae9dad4fc53bc748b3f121/index.html, slide What we want for P6)
- Under a possible path for P6, the slides state that blueprints instantiate the abstract model into multiple concrete representations for specific use cases and serialization targets. ^s3
  > "Blueprints instantiate the abstract model into multiple concrete representations for specific use cases and serialization targets." (https://github.com/TEIC/timeForP6/blob/eb924226d12d22599bae9dad4fc53bc748b3f121/index.html, slide A possible path for P6, About Blueprints)
- On the same slide, the slides state that the TEI Council would maintain a number of official blueprints. ^s4
  > "TEI Council would maintain a number of official blueprints." (https://github.com/TEIC/timeForP6/blob/eb924226d12d22599bae9dad4fc53bc748b3f121/index.html, slide A possible path for P6, About Blueprints)
- Under whether P6 is worth the trouble, the slides state that P5 can continue to develop, including adding new features, while P6 is being designed. ^s5
  > "P5 can continue to develop (including adding new features) while P6 is being designed." (https://github.com/TEIC/timeForP6/blob/eb924226d12d22599bae9dad4fc53bc748b3f121/index.html, slide Is this P6 worth the trouble?)
- Under the heading Experiments, the slides state that Council members do not yet agree on how to move forward. ^s6
  > "We don’t yet agree on how to move forward." (https://github.com/TEIC/timeForP6/blob/eb924226d12d22599bae9dad4fc53bc748b3f121/index.html, slide Experiments)
- On the same slide, the slides state a felt need to check in with the community before spending more time and resources. ^s7
  > "We feel the need to check in with the community before spending more time and resources." (https://github.com/TEIC/timeForP6/blob/eb924226d12d22599bae9dad4fc53bc748b3f121/index.html, slide Experiments)
