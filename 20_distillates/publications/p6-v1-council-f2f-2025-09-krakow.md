# Distillate: TEI Technical Council face-to-face meeting in Kraków, 14–16 September 2025

This citation-only distillate records selected P6 passages from the official Council minutes page for the Kraków face-to-face meeting, observed on 2026-09-11 with bytes identical to the 2026-09-04 census.

## Core statements

- In the discussion of the Council's readiness to move from P5 to P6, the minutes record that the Council considers XML technology to hold up well while the class system does not. ^s1
  > "Council feels that the XML technology still holds really well, but the class system doesn’t" (https://www.tei-c.org/activities/council/meetings/tei-technical-council-f2f-meeting-in-Krakow-14-16-sept-2025/, TEI Technical Council F2F Meeting in Kraków, 14–16 September 2025, Discussion of Council’s readiness to move from P5 to P6)
- In the same discussion, the minutes record the Council's wish to start prioritizing P6 development in spring 2026 and to begin shifting P5 toward maintenance-only support. ^s2
  > "Council would like to start in Spring 2026 to prioritize P6 development, and begin to shift to maintenance-only support of P5" (https://www.tei-c.org/activities/council/meetings/tei-technical-council-f2f-meeting-in-Krakow-14-16-sept-2025/, TEI Technical Council F2F Meeting in Kraków, 14–16 September 2025, Discussion of Council’s readiness to move from P5 to P6)
- The minutes record an aim to deliver a presentation about P6 at the 2026 TEI conference. ^s3
  > "We should aim to deliver a presentation about P6 at the 2026 TEI conference." (https://www.tei-c.org/activities/council/meetings/tei-technical-council-f2f-meeting-in-Krakow-14-16-sept-2025/, TEI Technical Council F2F Meeting in Kraków, 14–16 September 2025, Discussion of Council’s readiness to move from P5 to P6)
- The minutes note P6 as continuing with XML while changing the architecture and schemas. ^s4
  > "P6 to continue with XML but change the architecture / schemas." (https://www.tei-c.org/activities/council/meetings/tei-technical-council-f2f-meeting-in-Krakow-14-16-sept-2025/, TEI Technical Council F2F Meeting in Kraków, 14–16 September 2025, Discussion of Council’s readiness to move from P5 to P6)
