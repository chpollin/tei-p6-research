# Distillate: TEI Technical Council teleconference, 10 April 2026

This citation-only distillate records the meeting arrangement for P5 and P6 and the P5-to-P6 passages of the official minutes page of the 10 April 2026 Council teleconference, observed on 2026-09-11 with bytes identical to the 2026-09-04 census.

## Core statements

- The minutes record that the Council agrees to plan alternating P5 and P6 meetings. ^s1
  > "Council agrees to plan alternating P5 and P6 meetings." (https://www.tei-c.org/activities/council/meetings/tei-technical-council-teleconference-2026-04-10/, TEI Technical Council Teleconference, 2026-04-10, Next meetings)

  > "General agreement that the term "maintenance mode" does not apply until we are ready to release P6." (https://www.tei-c.org/activities/council/meetings/tei-technical-council-teleconference-2026-04-10/, TEI Technical Council Teleconference, 2026-04-10, P5 to P6)
- In the same discussion, the minutes state that the Council should start applying a label that marks tickets for P5 to P6. ^s3
  > "Council should start applying a label marking tickets for P5 -> P6." (https://www.tei-c.org/activities/council/meetings/tei-technical-council-teleconference-2026-04-10/, TEI Technical Council Teleconference, 2026-04-10, P5 to P6)

## Terms

## Appraisal

The page is a primary Council meeting record. Statement s1 records an agreement on meeting organization and s2 a recorded general agreement on terminology; s3 is phrased as what the Council should do and follows a participant's contribution without an attribution of its own, so it records a proposal or intention rather than an executed labelling practice. None of these passages establishes a P6 specification, implementation or release. The page body, including participant names, stays in local raw storage.
