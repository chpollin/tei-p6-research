# Distillate: TEI Technical Council P6 teleconference, 20 May 2026

This citation-only distillate records the passages on the visibility of the P6 sandbox repository in the official minutes page of the 20 May 2026 P6 teleconference, observed on 2026-09-11 with bytes identical to the 2026-09-04 census.

## Core statements

- Under P6 sandbox experiments, the minutes pose the question of when the P6 sandbox repository should be made public. ^s1
  > "P6 Sandbox repo: when should we make it public?" (https://www.tei-c.org/activities/council/meetings/tei-technical-council-p6-teleconference-2026-05-20/, TEI Technical Council P6 Teleconference, 2026-05-20, P6 Sandbox Experiments)
- In the P6 sandbox experiments section, the recorded Council discussion states that the P6 sandbox repository will be kept private for now while linked from the minutes. ^s2
  > "For now, we'll keep it private but link to it from our minutes." (https://www.tei-c.org/activities/council/meetings/tei-technical-council-p6-teleconference-2026-05-20/, TEI Technical Council P6 Teleconference, 2026-05-20, P6 Sandbox Experiments, Council discussion)
- The minutes state the intention to make the repository public eventually, ideally by the TEI 2026 conference when the Council shares its work with the community. ^s3
