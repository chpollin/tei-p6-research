# Quotation selection ambiguity

Quotation selection ambiguity arises when a target is addressed by quoting its text and the
quotation occurs more than once, so that the selection either covers every occurrence or
leaves open which single occurrence was intended. Whether a plural match is the intended
target or an unresolved choice belongs to this phenomenon.
