# Overlapping hierarchy

Overlapping hierarchy is the situation in which two or more structural readings of one
text, such as its division into pages and its division into paragraphs, cross each other so
that no single tree contains both. The question whether a text has one privileged hierarchy
at all is part of the same situation.
