# Foliation and page pointers

Foliation and page pointers record where the transcribed text crosses a boundary of the
physical carrier, through a page or folio number assigned by the encoder, a pointer to a
facsimile image, or a folio number transcribed from the page itself. These records are
independent of one another, and whether they align with each other and with the image is a
question of its own.
