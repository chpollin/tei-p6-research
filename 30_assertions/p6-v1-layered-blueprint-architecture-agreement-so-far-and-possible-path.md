# A participant's review in the July 2026 Council minutes lists a layered P6 architecture; Council session slides sketch a possible role for blueprints

## Statement

The minutes of the P6 teleconference of 16 July 2026 list, in a participant's review of the Council's points of agreement so far, that P6 should provide a layered architecture running from abstract model to blueprints to customizations or chained blueprints. The slide source of the Council's TEI 2026 session at `TEIC/timeForP6` commit `eb924226`, under a possible path for P6, states that blueprints instantiate the abstract model into multiple concrete representations for specific use cases and serialization targets. These are separately attributed descriptions; their equivalence, formal adoption and implementation require further evidence.
