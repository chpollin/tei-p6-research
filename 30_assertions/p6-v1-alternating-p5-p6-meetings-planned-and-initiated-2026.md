# Council minutes record a March 2026 plan for two monthly meetings and an April 2026 agreement and stated start of alternating P5 and P6 meetings.

## Statement

The minutes of the Würzburg face-to-face meeting of 10–13 March 2026 state that the Council will begin hosting two meetings per month to support P6 development while continuing work requested on P5. The minutes of 10 April 2026 record that the Council agrees to plan alternating P5 and P6 meetings. The minutes of 24 April 2026 state that this meeting initiates the Council's new bimonthly agenda to alternate working on P5 and P6. The assertion reports a planned and, in the minutes' own words, initiated meeting arrangement; it does not report what later meetings achieved.
