# In TEI P5 4.12.0, the Guidelines state that placeName may be regarded as an abbreviation for name or rs of type place and add in a footnote that strictly a value such as figurative should be given to the periphrastic place names in the placeName version of their example to preserve the distinction that the choice of rs had indicated

## Statement

In TEI P5 4.12.0, the Guidelines state that placeName, like persName, may be regarded simply as an abbreviation for name of type place or rs of type place, so that the two versions of their example are equivalent, and add in a footnote that strictly a value such as figurative should be given to the periphrastic place names in the placeName version to preserve the distinction that the choice of rs instead of name had indicated.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/documents/tei-p5-guidelines-nd-4.12.0#^s18]] — The equivalence claim together with the footnote that qualifies it for the periphrastic names of the example. The footnote establishes that in that example the specialized element by itself does not carry the distinction between a proper name and a referring string, and it states no general rule.
