# In TEI P5 4.12.0, the Guidelines reserve roleName for roles that function as part of a name and place information about a person's roles and occupations within person

## Statement

In TEI P5 4.12.0, the Guidelines use addName and roleName for name components other than those already listed in the section, distinguish roleName as enclosing an associated component such as an aristocratic or official title that exists in some sense independently of its bearer, and admit that this distinction is not always clear. They state that roleName is intended for roles that function as part of a person's name, so that the role a person has in a given context, such as witness or defendant in a legal document, stays outside it, and that information about a person's roles and occupations belongs within the person element.
