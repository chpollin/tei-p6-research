# In TEI P5 4.12.0, the description of evidence in att.editLike states that it indicates the nature of the evidence supporting the reliability or accuracy of the intervention or interpretation

## Statement

In TEI P5 4.12.0, the description of the evidence attribute in the attribute class att.editLike states that it indicates the nature of the evidence supporting the reliability or accuracy of the intervention or interpretation.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/documents/tei-p5-att.editlike-4.12.0#^s2]] — The attribute description at the pinned release. It establishes that the attribute qualifies an intervention or interpretation by the kind of evidence behind it, and it names neither the elements that carry the attribute nor what counts as an intervention or interpretation.
