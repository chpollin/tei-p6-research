# In TEI P5 4.12.0, the Guidelines group information about a person, as distinct from references to a person such as by name, within a person element

## Statement

In TEI P5 4.12.0, the Guidelines group information about a person, as distinct from references to a person, for example by name, within a person element. They state that information about a group of people regarded as a single entity, for example the audience of a performance, may be encoded using the personGrp element, and note that information about a group of people with a distinct identity, for example a named theatrical troupe, should be recorded using the org element.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/documents/tei-p5-guidelines-nd-4.12.0#^s26]] — The opening of the chapter's section on person records. It states the separation of the record from references to the person for the person element itself, in the chapter's own words, and it states nothing about how a reference reaches the record or about the place record.
