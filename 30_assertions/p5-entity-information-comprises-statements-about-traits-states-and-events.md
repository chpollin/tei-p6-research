# In TEI P5 4.12.0, the Guidelines describe information about people, places, organizations and events as statements about traits, states, events and external resources

## Statement

In TEI P5 4.12.0, the Guidelines state that information about people, places, organizations and events essentially comprises statements or assertions about traits, characteristics which by and large do not change over time, about states, characteristics which hold true only at a specific time, about events, incidents which may lead to a change of state or less frequently of trait, and about external resources where other information on the subject can be found.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/documents/tei-p5-guidelines-nd-4.12.0#^s21]] — The chapter's framing of biographical, organizational and geographical information as statements of four kinds. It establishes the vocabulary and states nothing about how a statement is attributed or dated.
