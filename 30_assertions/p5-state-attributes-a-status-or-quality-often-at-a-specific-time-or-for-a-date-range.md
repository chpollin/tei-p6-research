# In TEI P5 4.12.0, the English description of state states that the status or quality is attributed often at some specific time or for a specific date range

## Statement

In TEI P5 4.12.0, the English description of the element state states that the status or quality it describes is attributed often at some specific time or for a specific date range.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/documents/tei-p5-state-4.12.0#^s3]] — The element description at the pinned release. It establishes that the time frame of a state is stated as usual rather than required, with the word often, and it states nothing about the attributes that carry the time or the date range.
