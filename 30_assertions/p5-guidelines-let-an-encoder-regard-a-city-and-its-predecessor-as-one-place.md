# In TEI P5 4.12.0, the Guidelines relate the suggestion to regard Lyon and Lugdunum as the same place to their significant overlapping areas

## Statement

In TEI P5 4.12.0, the Guidelines state that the modern city of Lyon and the Roman Lugdunum are not physically co-extensive but have significant areas which overlap, and that an encoder may therefore wish to regard them as the same place while supplying both names with an indication of the time period during which each was current.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/documents/tei-p5-guidelines-nd-4.12.0#^s43]] — The chapter's example of the modern city of Lyon and the Roman Lugdunum. It presents regarding both as the same place as what an encoder may wish to do and places the indication of the time period on the two names.
