# In TEI P5 4.12.0, the Guidelines state that the association nymRef makes with a nym has nothing to do with any individual who might use the name

## Statement

In TEI P5 4.12.0, the Guidelines state that any element which is a member of att.naming may use the attribute nymRef to indicate the nym with which it corresponds, illustrate this with a nym for a full name form and an occurrence in running text encoded as a forename holding a familiar short form whose nymRef points to that nym, and note that this association has nothing to do with any individual who might use the name.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/documents/tei-p5-guidelines-nd-4.12.0#^s56]] — The statement in the section on nyms, with its example of a short form pointing to the nym of the full form. It establishes the independence of the association from any individual who might use the name, and it says nothing about places or organizations. The class description recorded in [[30_assertions/p5-att-naming-describes-nymref-through-the-object-named]] reaches the canonical form through the object named instead.
