# In TEI P5 4.12.0, the Guidelines state that the association nymRef makes with a nym has nothing to do with any individual who might use the name

## Statement

In TEI P5 4.12.0, the Guidelines state that any element which is a member of att.naming may use the attribute nymRef to indicate the nym with which it corresponds, illustrate this with a nym for the name "Antony" and an occurrence in running text encoded as a forename reading "Tony" whose nymRef points to that nym, and note that this association has nothing to do with any individual who might use the name.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/documents/tei-p5-guidelines-nd-4.12.0#^s56]] — The chapter's statement with its example of the forename "Tony" pointing to the nym for "Antony". It states the independence of the association from any individual who might use the name and says nothing about places or organizations. The vault holds this assertion as contested with [[30_assertions/p5-att-naming-describes-nymref-through-the-object-named]].
