# In TEI P5 4.12.0, the Guidelines state that their mechanisms for marking personal name components will not cater for every personal name or every processing need, and recommend feature structures where the structure is highly complex or the components particularly ambiguous

## Statement

In TEI P5 4.12.0, the Guidelines state that the mechanisms for marking the components of personal names, although highly flexible, will not cater for every personal name nor for every processing need, and recommend feature structures as the most appropriate mechanism where the internal structure of personal names is highly complex or where name components are particularly ambiguous.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/documents/tei-p5-guidelines-nd-4.12.0#^s12]] — The limit the chapter states for its personal name markup and the conditional recommendation of feature structures. It speaks of personal names alone.
