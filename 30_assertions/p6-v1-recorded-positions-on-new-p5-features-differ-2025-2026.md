# Official P6 records between September 2025 and August 2026 state differing positions on how far P5 continues to develop while P6 is worked on.

## Statement

In September 2025 the Kraków minutes record the Council's wish to prioritize P6 development from spring 2026 and to begin shifting P5 toward maintenance-only support. In October 2025 the minutes record a Council decision to continue with P5 feature requests for now, until a proposed discussion. In March 2026 the Würzburg minutes list announcing at the Annual Members' Meeting that new features stop until P6 starts. In April 2026 the minutes record general agreement that the term maintenance mode does not apply until the Council is ready to release P6. In July 2026 the minutes state that the speed of P5 updates may decrease as energy goes to P6. The TEI 2026 panel slides at commit `eb924226` state that P5 can continue to develop, including adding new features, while P6 is being designed. These records have different kinds, namely a wish, a labelled decision, a planned announcement, a terminological agreement, a review statement and presentation slides. The assertion reports the recorded positions; it does not establish which handling of feature requests was applied in P5 releases.
