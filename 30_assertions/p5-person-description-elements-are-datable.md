# In TEI P5 4.12.0, the Guidelines state that all the specific and generic elements under discussion are members of att.datable and can therefore be limited in terms of time

## Statement

In TEI P5 4.12.0, the Guidelines state that all these elements, specific and generic, are members of att.datable and can therefore be limited in terms of time, and illustrate this with a person holding one persName with notAfter and another with notBefore for a person who changed name in a given year.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/documents/tei-p5-guidelines-nd-4.12.0#^s36]] — The class membership as the chapter states it, for the set of elements its surrounding text has introduced, with a name form as the dated element in the example. The statement does not enumerate that set, and the class specification of att.datable is not among the sources.
