# Council minutes of September 2025, March 2026 and July 2026 and the Council's TEI 2026 panel slides present continued XML use as a P6 direction.

## Statement

The Kraków minutes of 14–16 September 2025 note P6 as continuing with XML while changing architecture and schemas. The Würzburg minutes of 10–13 March 2026, in notes on the questions planned for the P6 panel proposal, state that the Council is not breaking from XML. The minutes of 16 July 2026 list XML as staying the primary format in a participant's review of points of agreement so far. The slide source of the Council's TEI 2026 panel at `TEIC/timeForP6` commit `eb924226` states that TEI will still be encoded in XML. The Würzburg minutes, the July review and the slides pair this with expressing the abstract model or TEI in other forms, naming RDF, graph models or JSON-LD. The assertion reports a position stated in a note, planning notes, a review list and presentation slides; it reports no adopted specification.
