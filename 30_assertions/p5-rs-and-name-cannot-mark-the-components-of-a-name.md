# In TEI P5 4.12.0, the Guidelines state that rs and name are insufficiently powerful to mark the internal components or structure of names and provide persName, surname, forename, roleName, addName, nameLink and genName for these and related purposes

## Statement

In TEI P5 4.12.0, the Guidelines state that the core rs and name elements can distinguish names in a text but are insufficiently powerful to mark their internal components or structure, that distinguishing a family name, a forename and an honorary title matters for nominal record linkage or even for an alphabetically sorted list of personal names, and that the elements persName, surname, forename, roleName, addName, nameLink and genName are provided for these and related purposes.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/documents/tei-p5-guidelines-nd-4.12.0#^s7]] — The chapter's motivation for the personal name elements. It establishes the stated limit of the core elements and lists the seven elements on one level, and it states nothing about the content model of persName or about which of the elements are its components.
